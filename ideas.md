**Design Movement**: Brutalist Concert Poster Aesthetic

**Core Principles**:
- Raw, bold typography with intentional asymmetry
- High-contrast black backgrounds with stark white and electric accent colors
- Layered, overlapping elements that create visual tension
- Exposed structural elements (visible grids, raw edges, unpolished textures)

**Color Philosophy**: 
Deep black foundations (#0a0a0a) with electric cyan (#00ffff) and hot magenta (#ff00ff) accents to evoke stage lighting and sound waves. The palette is intentionally harsh and energetic, mirroring the intensity of live performances. White text provides stark contrast for readability while maintaining the raw aesthetic.

**Layout Paradigm**: 
Diagonal cuts and angled sections dominate the page structure. Content blocks are positioned at unconventional angles, creating a sense of movement and energy. The curtain opening reveals a stage set at a 15-degree tilt, breaking traditional horizontal layouts.

**Signature Elements**:
- Glitch effects on hover states that simulate audio distortion
- Waveform visualizations as decorative borders
- Torn paper edges between sections to represent stage curtains
- Neon glow effects on interactive elements

**Interaction Philosophy**: 
Every interaction should feel like triggering a sound effect. Buttons pulse with energy, hover states create visual "reverb," and the curtain opening is a dramatic, theatrical moment with sound-inspired motion curves.

**Animation**: 
Heavy use of cubic-bezier easing to create snappy, percussive movements. The curtain opens with a dramatic ease-out that mimics the weight of real velvet. Elements enter with staggered timing, like instruments joining a performance. Subtle continuous animations (floating, pulsing) maintain energy throughout the page.

**Typography System**: 
Display font: "Bebas Neue" or "Oswald" (bold, condensed, all-caps for headers)
Body font: "Space Grotesk" or "DM Sans" (geometric, modern, highly legible)
Hierarchy: Massive hero text (clamp(3rem, 8vw, 8rem)), medium section headers (2-3rem), compact body text (1rem-1.125rem)

**Design Movement**: Vintage Audio Equipment Catalog

**Core Principles**:
- Warm, analog color palette inspired by 1970s hi-fi equipment
- Tactile textures: brushed metal, wood grain, leather accents
- Precise alignment and grid-based layouts that echo technical schematics
- Nostalgic references to classic audio gear (VU meters, knobs, dials)

**Color Philosophy**: 
Rich walnut browns (#3e2723, #5d4037) paired with brushed gold (#b8860b) and copper accents (#cd7f32). Background uses cream (#f5f5dc) and warm off-whites to simulate aged paper. The palette evokes the warmth of vintage tube amplifiers and the craftsmanship of classic audio equipment.

**Layout Paradigm**: 
Modular grid system inspired by equipment rack layouts. Sections are organized like components in a mixing console—horizontal strips with clear divisions. The curtain opening reveals a stage designed like the front panel of a vintage amplifier, with the logo centered like a brand plate.

**Signature Elements**:
- VU meter animations that respond to scroll position
- Circular knob controls as navigation elements
- Embossed text effects on headers
- Stitched leather texture dividers between sections

**Interaction Philosophy**: 
Interactions should feel mechanical and precise, like turning a physical knob or sliding a fader. Smooth, controlled animations with slight resistance. The curtain opening has a mechanical quality, as if operated by a motorized system.

**Animation**: 
Smooth, linear transitions with subtle easing. VU meter needles bounce with realistic physics. Elements slide in from the sides like rack-mounted equipment being installed. Hover states include subtle scale and shadow changes to simulate depth.

**Typography System**: 
Display font: "Playfair Display" or "Libre Baskerville" (serif, elegant, classic)
Body font: "Lato" or "Source Sans Pro" (clean, professional, readable)
Hierarchy: Elegant large headers (3-4rem), balanced subheaders (1.5-2rem), comfortable body text (1.125rem)
</text>

**Design Movement**: Cyberpunk Stage Tech

**Core Principles**:
- Neon-lit darkness with holographic UI elements
- Futuristic, tech-forward aesthetic with sci-fi influences
- Layered transparency and glass-morphism effects
- Digital grid patterns and circuit-board inspired layouts

**Color Philosophy**: 
Deep space black (#0d0d0d) as the foundation, with electric purple (#9d4edd), neon green (#39ff14), and cyan blue (#00d9ff) creating a vibrant, futuristic palette. The colors represent digital signals and electronic soundwaves. Subtle gradients add depth and dimension, while glowing effects simulate LED lighting.

**Layout Paradigm**: 
Floating panels with glass-morphism effects create a layered, 3D interface. The curtain opening reveals a holographic stage projection with the logo appearing as a 3D light construct. Diagonal grid lines and perspective transforms give the illusion of depth and digital space.

**Signature Elements**:
- Scanline effects that move across sections
- Hexagonal grid patterns as background textures
- Holographic gradient borders on cards and buttons
- Particle effects that trail cursor movement

**Interaction Philosophy**: 
Every interaction should feel like interfacing with advanced technology. Buttons emit light pulses on click, hover states trigger holographic reveals, and the curtain opening is a digital materialization effect with particle dispersion.

**Animation**: 
Fast, snappy animations with elastic easing for playful energy. The curtain dissolves into particles rather than sliding. Elements fade in with staggered delays and slight position shifts. Continuous subtle animations (floating, glowing, pulsing) maintain the futuristic atmosphere.

**Typography System**: 
Display font: "Orbitron" or "Rajdhani" (geometric, futuristic, tech-inspired)
Body font: "Exo 2" or "Chakra Petch" (modern, clean, slightly geometric)
Hierarchy: Bold, uppercase headers (clamp(2.5rem, 6vw, 6rem)), medium subheaders (1.75-2.5rem), crisp body text (1rem)
</text>
